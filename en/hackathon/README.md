# Hackathon

Welcome to the participant guide for the current global hackathon cycle, presented by Colosseum! This guide provides all the information you need to participate, compete, and succeed, with dedicated support from Superteam Brasil.

## Current Edition: Crypto World's Fair

The **Crypto World's Fair** ([colosseum.com/worldsfair](https://colosseum.com/worldsfair)) is Colosseum's open online competition bringing together builders and founders across all crypto ecosystems, with Solana as one of the dedicated tracks. Colosseum's hackathons have produced 60k+ builders and 9k+ products; roughly 85% of VC-funded Solana startups came out of these competitions.

- **Period**: September 14 – October 12, 2026 (100% online, free, open to anyone anywhere)
- **Submission deadline**: October 12, 2026, at 23h59 (Brasília time)
- **Start here (Brazil)**: [hackathon.superteam.com.br](https://hackathon.superteam.com.br), the Superteam Brasil landing page with the full participation flow
- **Registration (global)**: [colosseum.com/worldsfair](https://colosseum.com/worldsfair)
- **Year-round**: the Eternal Challenge ([colosseum.com/eternal](https://colosseum.com/eternal)) lets you launch a product in 4 weeks, any time; winners advance to the accelerator with $250k pre-seed funding.

You don't need prior blockchain experience, an idea, or a team to join. The community helps with team formation, workshops and mentorship run throughout September and October, and designers, product, marketing, and business profiles are welcome. See [How to Participate](how-to-participate.md).

## Prizes (Summary)

- **Total prizes**: **$840,000** announced by Colosseum, combining $340k in global awards (the $30k Grand Prize, $300k shared across the next 20 best projects at $15k each, the $5k Public Good Prize, and the $5k University Prize) with the dedicated ecosystem track pools
- **Solana track**: $100,000, with 10 projects receiving $10,000 each
- **Colosseum Accelerator**: **$2.5 million** in seed funding. Accepted teams receive a $250,000 pre-seed check each, plus 12 weeks in San Francisco with 1:1 mentorship and a Demo Day
- **Brazil Track**: US$5,000 (R$25k) in additional prizes plus perks, exclusive to Brazilian participants, published on [Superteam Earn](https://superteam.fun/earn/s/superteambr)
